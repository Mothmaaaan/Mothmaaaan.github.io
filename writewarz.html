Major Contributions

Common Themes

  • I was in charge of creating the encrypted word system in the Apocalyptic theme as well as creating the input and word recognition systems for Word Zombies.
  • I created the Goblins Attack minigame for the Fantasy theme.
  • I created the Word Guess minigame for the Sci-Fi theme.
  • I created the Lexicon Hold 'Em minigame for the Western theme.

System Reworks

  • Write Warz looks and plays completely different than when I started back in July. Almost every major system has gone through a make-over:
  • Most recently, I completely overhauled how the voting and tie-breaker sequences play out due to internal and external feedback.
  • The writing state of the game has changed to increase the readability of the current story and your current typed sentence.
  • I implemented a new marketplace to align with a new monetization model.
  • I setup a new system for choosing and loading your custom game settings.
  • I was responsible for many small fixes and tweaks to elevate the player experience and eliminate bugs.
